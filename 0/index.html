Becoming Friends with Your Camera

Perspective, focal length, and where the camera stands.

Selfie

Close up without zoom, then from several feet back with zoom, keeping the face the same size.

Up close, the nose is much nearer to the lens than the ears, so it projects larger. Stepping back evens out those distances, and zooming only scales the image.

Architecture

Far away with zoom, then walked up without zoom, keeping the building the same size.

From far away, near and far parts of the scene are at nearly the same distance, so depth flattens. Up close, the ratio of those distances is large and the scene looks deep.

Dolly zoom

Moving the camera back while zooming in, so the subject stays the same size and the background stretches.
